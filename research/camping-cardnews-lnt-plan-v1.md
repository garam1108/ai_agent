# Card News Plan: "Camping Without a Trace" (LNT 7 Principles & Eco Camping)

## 1. Overview

- **Topic:** Camping
- **Selected plan:** #5 - Leave No Trace (LNT) 7 principles and eco camping
- **Format:** Card news, 8 cards total (Card 1 = thumbnail)
- **Target audience:** Campers interested in the environment and value-driven consumption; beginners who want to camp with good manners
- **Core message:** A camper borrows nature for a night. Leave it as you found it.
- **Tone:** Friendly, short, action-oriented ("Do this" rather than "Don't do that")
- **Suggested channel:** Instagram (4:5 or 1:1 cards)

## 2. Background Research Summary

- LNT (Leave No Trace) started with a US national park environmental movement. Its goal is to minimize human impact on nature. Rule of thumb: take back every piece of trash you brought and leave no sign of your visit.
- Trash and fire traces are still found at popular backpacking sites, which also fuels conflict between user groups.
- Some campgrounds ban firewood or charcoal because of fire risk, so campers must check rules before booking.
- Many places such as parks and seawalls legally prohibit camping or overnight car stays (car camping), so location must be checked first.
- Fire safety: pour water around the fire and fully put out embers.
- Trend context: "bulmeong" (campfire gazing), "campnic" (camping picnic in a nearby park), car camping, and minimal camping are growing, so more people share the same natural spaces.
- Note: No Korean camping population statistic was verified. Do not put a number on the cards until it is confirmed from Korea Tourism Organization or Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism data.

## 3. Card Structure (8 Cards)

### Card 1 - Thumbnail
- **Headline:** Leave nothing but footprints
- **Sub-copy:** 7 LNT rules for campers who want to keep camping
- **Visual:** A tent at dusk in a clean forest; a single line of footprints; large headline with high contrast
- **Purpose:** Stop the scroll with a warm, clear promise

### Card 2 - Why It Matters
- **Headline:** Campsites are not a trash can
- **Body:**
  - Trash and fire traces remain even at famous sites.
  - Some campgrounds already restrict fires because of the damage.
  - If we keep leaving traces, the places we love will close.
- **Visual:** Split image: a clean site vs. a littered site

### Card 3 - What Is LNT?
- **Headline:** LNT = Leave No Trace
- **Body:**
  - A movement that started with US national park environmental groups.
  - One idea: reduce your impact on nature.
  - Seven simple principles, shown on the next cards.
- **Visual:** Seven small icons in a row (one per principle)

### Card 4 - Principles 1-2
- **Headline:** Plan well, camp on solid ground
- **Body:**
  1. **Plan ahead and prepare:** Check camping rules, fire rules, and whether camping is allowed at the place before you go. Repack food to reduce packaging waste.
  2. **Travel and camp on durable surfaces:** Use designated sites or hardened ground. Do not trample grass or flatten new spots.
- **Visual:** Map pin with a check mark; tent on a hard-packed pad

### Card 5 - Principles 3-4
- **Headline:** Take it all back, leave it all there
- **Body:**
  3. **Dispose of waste properly:** Pack out all trash, including food scraps and cigarette butts. Do not dump wastewater near streams.
  4. **Leave what you find:** Do not pick plants, move rocks, or take natural souvenirs. Photos are the only souvenir.
- **Visual:** Trash bag next to a backpack; a flower with a camera icon

### Card 6 - Principles 5-6
- **Headline:** Fire and wildlife need respect
- **Body:**
  5. **Minimize campfire impacts:** Follow campground fire rules. Use a fire pit or fire stand, pour water around the fire, and fully put out embers.
  6. **Respect wildlife:** Observe from a distance. Do not feed animals. Seal food and trash so animals do not get used to humans.
- **Visual:** Campfire stand with a water bucket; a deer at a safe distance

### Card 7 - Principle 7 + Daily Eco Habits
- **Headline:** Be kind to your neighbors
- **Body:**
  7. **Be considerate of other visitors:** Keep noise low at night, keep lights from shining at other sites, and keep pets under control.
  - **Eco tips:** Reusable cups and containers, a trash bag in the car, and a small kit for picking up extra litter.
- **Visual:** Quiet night site with warm lights; small eco kit flat lay

### Card 8 - Summary and Call to Action
- **Headline:** Save this and share it before your next trip
- **Body:**
  - Recap of the seven principles in one line each.
  - Call to action: Save, share with your camping buddy, and comment "my one trace-free habit".
- **Visual:** Checklist of 7 items with check marks; brand/handle area

## 4. Design Direction

- **Mood:** Natural and calm, with one accent color for emphasis
- **Palette suggestion:** Forest green, warm beige, charcoal text, orange accent (campfire)
- **Typography:** Bold sans-serif headlines; body text at least 28 px on a 1080 px wide card
- **Layout rules:** One idea per card; keep text inside safe margins; no overlap between text and image focal points
- **Consistency:** Same icon style for the 7 principles; page numbers (1/8 to 8/8) on every card except the thumbnail
