# Slide Design System: Clean Teal Layout

Design reference for building slides. Source: "Clean layout theme PPT" template image (15 sample slides).

## 1. Tone

Clean, corporate, trustworthy. Flat vector style, generous white space, one dominant teal hue with a single yellow accent.

## 2. Colors

| Role | HEX | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Primary teal | `#00877A` | Title bars, key shapes, cover/closing background |
| Dark teal | `#006B63` | Shadows, layered shapes, headings on light bg |
| Light teal | `#4DB6AC` | Secondary shapes, rings, chart bars |
| Pale teal | `#E0F2F1` | Soft panels, icon circles |
| Accent yellow | `#F5B700` | Highlights only (donut segments, active dots) |
| Navy | `#1F3A6E` | Rare emphasis headline text |
| Dark gray | `#333333` | Body text, titles on white |
| Mid gray | `#808080` | Sub-text, chart bars, captions |
| Light gray | `#F2F2F2` | Content-slide background panels |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main background, text on teal |

Rules: teal family covers about 80% of color use; yellow stays under 5%; never add other hues.

## 3. Typography

- Korean: **Noto Sans KR** (fallback: Malgun Gothic). Latin: same family or Arial.
- Title: 28-36 pt, Bold. Cover title up to 40 pt.
- Section/item heading ("main title"): 16-20 pt, Bold.
- Body: 11-14 pt, Regular, dark gray. Minimum 10 pt.
- Caption / footer: 8-10 pt, mid gray.
- Text on teal is white; text on white or gray is dark gray.
- Max 2 weights per slide (Bold + Regular).

## 4. Layout

- Ratio 16:9 (13.333 x 7.5 in). Margins at least 0.5 in on every side.
- Header: slide title at top left or top center, thin gray subtitle line below, small logo at top right (diamond mark + "MY LOGO").
- Footer: small logo or URL at the bottom, optional teal strip along the bottom edge.
- Use a 12-column grid; keep content blocks aligned and evenly spaced.
- One message per slide; at most 6 repeated items (icons, steps, columns).

### Slide types

| Type | Composition |
|------|-------------|
| Cover | White left half with title + subtitle + URL; large wavy teal shape on the right/top |
| Closing | Full teal background, centered "Thank You" in white, wavy lighter band |
| Hub / cycle | Center circle with 4-6 satellites linked by thin lines, icon + title + 2 lines text each |
| Steps / process | 3-6 numbered items in a row or column (circles, ribbons, arrows, "STEP 01") |
| Ribbon banner | Teal ribbon with folded ends holding the slide title |
| Comparison / list | Left teal labels, right text with icons; or 2x2 icon-text grid |
| Chart | Simple bar/donut charts in teal + gray, yellow for the highlighted value, large numerals (01, 02, 03) |
| Device mockup | Monitor or phone image beside 2-3 text items |

## 5. Shapes and icons

- Flat fills only; no outlines heavier than 1 pt, no gradients, subtle drop shadow at most.
- Wavy/organic curves for cover and closing; straight angled bands for content slides.
- Circles for icons and step numbers; icons are simple white line/glyph icons on teal circles.
- Dividers: thin 0.75 pt gray lines.

## 6. Do / Don't

- Do keep text short (titles under 20 characters, descriptions under 2 lines).
- Do keep consistent spacing, alignment, and repeated header/footer on every content slide.
- Don't use more than teal + gray + one yellow accent.
- Don't let text overflow shapes or overlap other elements.
- Don't use decorative clip art or photos with busy backgrounds.
