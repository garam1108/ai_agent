# LEGODT (레고트) - Official Store Facts (v2)

Source: official store https://legodt.kr/ ("LEGODT 레고트 공식 스토어"), checked 2026-11-01. This file supplements `legodt.md` (v1), whose reseller-based figures are superseded where they differ. Only facts (names, capacities, prices, materials, hold times) are recorded; official images and copy must not be reused.

## Store structure
- Main categories: New, Tumbler, Life, Bag, Travel, plus Tumggu and Pokemon collaboration lines.
- Tumbler category also sells parts: sleeves, DIY sleeves, DIY charms, straps (Short/Long), module lids, module bodies.
- Many items show SOLD OUT or Coming Soon on the check date. The store ships worldwide (country list includes Korea and Japan).
- "Make Your Own" section on the home page promotes customization.

## Features stated by the official store
- Module: 2-way drinking structure, handle can be removed and the handle joint closed with a silicone plug, wide mouth, silicone sleeve that reduces heat transfer to the hand, silicone bottom pad (less noise, anti-slip).
- Multi: easy-to-wash silicone sleeve and multi lid.
- Mini: sealed screw lid "to reduce leaks".
- Care: dishwasher not allowed; fill below the lid line; preheat with hot or cold water for 1 minute; avoid milk, dairy, juice and carbonated drinks for long storage; Tritan: no microwave, oven, freezer or dishwasher.
- Lid and body are different materials, so slight color differences are not defects.
