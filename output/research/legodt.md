# LEGODT (Tumbler Brand)

Research date: 2026-11-01

## Summary

- "legodt" most likely means LEGODT (Korean: 레고트, Japanese: レゴト / レゴット), a Korean modular and customizable tumbler brand. It has no relation to the LEGO Group or LEGO "Batmobile Tumbler" sets, which dominate generic search results.
- Origin is described by Korean retailer 29CM as a contemporary lifestyle brand from the ceramics specialist Odense. A May-August 2026 Korean article describes LEGODT as a CJ OnStyle lifestyle brand. These two descriptions are not reconciled in the sources (see "Unverified").
- Lineup is built around the "Loop Tumbler" series (Multi, Handy, Module, Tritan) in about 400-1000 ml. Reported Korean prices are about 32,000-36,000 KRW.
- Selling points: swap-and-decorate parts (28 parts on the Module), charms and stickers, 6-hour hot/cold retention, spill resistance, ergonomic handle, and character collaborations (Disney Zootopia, Manggom).
- Reported sales: about +45% year-on-year in June (2026) driven by the modular tumbler trend (single source). No independent customer reviews were found.

## Details

### 1. Disambiguation
- Generic searches for "legodt" or "레고DT 텀블러" return LEGO products (e.g. the LEGO Batman "The Tumbler" Batmobile set, LEGO "Tumbler with Drinking Straw" cup). These are unrelated.
- Interpretation chosen: LEGODT, the Korean drinkware brand. Confidence is high because the name, "tumbler", and the Loop Tumbler product line match across Korean, Japanese, and Singapore retail sources.
- Name: the Japanese release gives レゴト (Legoto) and does not explain the name. The brand icon/concept is "TUMGGU" (タムク), a coined word from Korean "tumbler" + "kkumida" (to decorate).

### 2. Brand overview and origin
- Origin: South Korea. 29CM describes it as a contemporary lifestyle brand from Odense, a ceramics specialist, offering playful everyday products with intuitive, colorful designs to help people express personality.
- Other description: a Money Today (English edition, Aug 2026) article calls LEGODT a lifestyle brand under CJ OnStyle, which reported its sales. Launch date and parent-company details are not stated in any source.
- Odense link is also repeated by a Japanese BUYMA listing and a desertcart listing ("odense legodt loop tumbler").
- International: Japan (official site legodt.jp, operated/promoted with Manamana Co., Ltd. as pop-up organizer) and Singapore (Takashimaya 10% off LEGODT promotion, September 2025).

### 4. Key features and selling points
- Customization: swappable parts, charms, stickers; "choosing each part is itself a consumption experience" (Money Today).
- Insulation: Japanese release claims thermos-level hot/cold retention, up to 6 hours (brand claim, not independently tested).
- Spill resistance when tipped, ergonomic handle (same release).
- Design: bright pop colors plus neutral colors for office, fitness, and park use.
- Collaborations: Disney Zootopia, Mangoom (Olive Young); the release says collaboration items sold out the same day (brand claim).
- Pop-up purchase benefits (Japan): 3 free decorating stickers; key-charm making experience with tumbler plus one item.

### 5. Target customers
- Not officially defined in the sources. The Money Today article places the modular trend among the MZ generation (Millennials and Gen Z). The Japanese release calls it popular among young Koreans.
- Interpretation: young adults (teens to 30s) who value personalization, character collabs, and "tumbler decorating" culture (office, gym, campus).

### 6. Brand tone and identity
- Playful, colorful, pop, "play while you choose" (Japanese pop-up title: "Choose your color like playing").
- Keywords: customizing, personality expression, lifestyle, intuitive design.
- Representative company quote (Money Today): will continue to introduce customization experiences reflecting consumers' tastes and lifestyles.

### 7. Official site and sales channels
- Japan official site: https://legodt.jp/ (not readable during research); Instagram @legodt.japan.
- Korea: Olive Young (sold-out claims; collab items), 29CM brand page (no products registered at time of check), CJ OnStyle (reported). KREAM lists Odense brand items.
- Japan: pop-ups at Hankyu Umeda, Shibuya Modi, and Grand Tree Musashikosugi (Nov 12-19, 2025); BUYMA resale.
- Singapore: Takashimaya (Sept 2025 promotion).
- A Korean official site/Instagram was not confirmed in this research.

### 8. Reviews and reputation
- No independent consumer reviews or tests were found. Only brand/press claims: sell-outs at major Olive Young stores, +45% YoY sales in June (2026), expansion via pop-ups in Japan.
- Treat reputation claims as unverified marketing/press statements.

## Unverified / gaps
- Parent company: Odense (29CM, BUYMA) vs CJ OnStyle (Money Today) - possibly Odense's brand sold via CJ OnStyle, but no source confirms this.
- Official site contents, official prices, launch year, and Korean official channels not retrieved.
- Prices are tracker/reseller values dated mid-2026 and may have changed.
- Material details for steel models and the Tritan lid/body split come from search summaries, not a fully read page.
- No independent reviews.
